ABOUT THIS SITE

Local AI on a personal computer

Models Hub is a student website about compact language models that people can try on their own computers.

Why this topic?

Small models are easier to compare on everyday hardware. This site introduces a few options and explains the first Ollama commands.

How to use the site

Start at the model directory, open a model detail page, and try the same prompt on more than one model.

Keep a critical view

Models can sound confident and still be wrong. Check important facts and use your own judgement.
